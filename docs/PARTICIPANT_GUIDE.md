# How to Take Part — Participant Guide

A 4-step walkthrough for joining the SignalLock OSINT & password-risk study. The
whole thing takes ~20 minutes. Everything you do is **voluntary** and your details
are returned **privately**, never committed to this repository.

> **One rule above all:** the study passwords you provide must be **throwaway** —
> never a password you actually use anywhere. The public details you give should be
> things that are **already public** about you.

## Step 1 — Read the information sheet
Open **[`STUDY_INFORMATION_SHEET.md`](STUDY_INFORMATION_SHEET.md)**. It explains what
the study is, what we collect (and what we never collect), how your data is
protected, and your right to withdraw.

## Step 2 — Give consent
Open **[`CONSENT_TEMPLATE.md`](CONSENT_TEMPLATE.md)**, fill it in for yourself, tick
the source types you're comfortable with, and sign it (typing your name + date is
fine). Keep a copy; send one to the researcher.

## Step 3 — Fill the intake form
Open **[`PARTICIPANT_INTAKE_FORM.md`](PARTICIPANT_INTAKE_FORM.md)**. Copy it into a
private document and fill in only the fields that are **already public** about you.
Leave anything blank that you don't want to share or that isn't public — partial is
fine.

## Step 4 — Choose study passwords & send it back
At the end of the intake form, invent **5–6 throwaway study passwords** following the
guidance there (some that relate to your public details, some that don't — this is
the heart of the experiment). Then send the completed **consent form + intake form**
privately to:

> **`sagarkishore.7@gmail.com`** (or whatever private channel the researcher gave you)

**Do not** open a pull request or commit your personal details to this public repo.

## What happens to your submission?

1. The researcher records your consent and gives you a **non-identifying id**
   (e.g. `participant-03`).
2. Your public details become *derived, typed facts* (a pet name, a year — never raw
   pages); your real handle stays only in an offline consent record.
3. The analysis measures how much your public footprint lowers the guessing effort
   for your study passwords. **Only risk bands/scores are stored — never a plaintext
   password.**
4. Findings are reported in aggregate; you are never individually identified.

## FAQ

- **Will you attack my real accounts?** No. The only "attack" is a bounded
  simulation against the throwaway study passwords you provide.
- **Do you need my real password?** No — never. Only throwaway study passwords.
- **Can I change my mind?** Yes, anytime — email the researcher and everything of
  yours is deleted (`STUDY_INFORMATION_SHEET.md` → Withdrawal).
- **Do I need to install anything?** No. You only fill in two text forms.
